# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

GitSeal contributors, maintainers, and community participants commit to a
welcoming, safe, and professional environment regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression,
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or other protected
characteristic.

Security review often involves disagreement. Critique evidence, designs, and
code; do not attack the person presenting them.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Welcome questions and explain security assumptions without gatekeeping.
- Credit research and disclose conflicts of interest.
- Protect private vulnerability reports and personal information.
- Correct mistakes openly and distinguish verified facts from hypotheses.
- Respect project boundaries, including the prohibition on testing systems
  without authorization.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misrepresentation, sustained disruption, or publishing another person's private
information is unacceptable. So is using project access to conceal a security
issue, retaliate against a reporter, or pressure someone to disclose sensitive
details publicly.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, reviews, issue trackers, community
spaces, and when representing the project publicly. Maintainers may remove or
edit contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or ban
participants when behavior threatens people or the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through an available private
GitHub channel. If none is available, create a minimal public request for a
private channel without naming people or describing sensitive events. The
project currently publishes no dedicated conduct email address. Maintainers
involved in a report must recuse themselves from handling it. Reports should be
handled with confidentiality, impartiality, and only the disclosure needed to
investigate and act.

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
